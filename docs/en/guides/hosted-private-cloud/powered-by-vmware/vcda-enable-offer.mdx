## Objective

OVHcloud provides a migration option based on **VMware Cloud Director Availability (VCDA)** to move your virtual machines to a Public VCF as-a-Service organisation.

**This guide explains how to enable this option from your OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- An active [Public VCF as-a-Service](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware-vcd) organisation.
- Access to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink> with administrator rights on the organisation.
- The public IP address (CIDR `/32`) of the server hosting your on-premises VCDA appliance.

:::info
The IP entered during activation is used to identify your source environment during configuration of the OVHcloud VCDA service.
:::

## Instructions

### Step 1: Open the Public VCF as-a-Service service

From the **Hosted Private Cloud** menu, click **Public VCF as-a-Service**.

The landing page shows your organisations list. Select the organisation where you want to enable migration.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Public VCF as-a-Service page with the organisations list" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-enable-offer/01-landing-public-vcf-en.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 2: Start activation

Once inside the organisation, open the dashboard and click <code className="action">Order</code> in the **Migration** block.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration block before activation with the Order button" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-enable-offer/02-enable-vcda-button-en.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 3: Fill in the first activation form

In the activation form, enter the public IP address of your source server in CIDR `/32` format, then click <code className="action">Order</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="VCDA activation form with the public IP field" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-enable-offer/03-first-activation-form-en.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
Make sure you enter the actual public egress IP used by your on-premises VCDA appliance. An incorrect IP will prevent pairing with the cloud service.
:::

:::info
The migration feature activation is handled by OVHcloud during delivery. You will receive a confirmation email once the option has been enabled on your organisation.
:::

### Step 4: Use the Migration tab after activation

Once the option is enabled, a <code className="action">Migration</code> tab appears in the organisation interface. You can then configure and launch your migrations to your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="New Migration tab after VCDA activation" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-enable-offer/04-migration-tab-en.png" loading="lazy" />
