## Objective

OVHcloud Connect Direct suits organisations already present in an OVHcloud PoP, or able to reach one, that manage their own physical cross-connect. It delivers a dedicated Layer 2 and Layer 3 connection to OVHcloud. Placing the order starts delivery: OVHcloud issues a Letter of Authorization (LOA), you (or your data centre operator) run the physical cross-connect, and the service then appears in the OVHcloud Control Panel for configuration.

OVHcloud Connect Direct is ordered from the [OVHcloud website](https://www.ovhcloud.com/). Once the order is validated and the service is delivered, it appears in the OVHcloud Control Panel, where you configure it (BGP, static routing, vRack association, and so on).

## What you will need

Before starting the order, prepare the following:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| **PoP location** | The data centre where you want to connect (see [PoP Locations](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/pop-locations-regions)) |
| **Bandwidth** | The speed of the connection (e.g. 1, 10 or 100 Gbps) |
| **Your ASN** | Your Autonomous System Number (public or private) for BGP peering |
| **IP plan** | The prefixes you intend to advertise and the peering subnet |
| **Contact details** | Technical and billing contacts for the order |
| **Cross-connect instructions** | Cage/cabinet references if you have your own space in the data centre |

## Place your order

OVHcloud Connect Direct is ordered from the [OVHcloud website](https://www.ovhcloud.com/), not from the Control Panel. On the website, select the OVHcloud Connect Direct offer, choose your **PoP location** and **bandwidth** (1, 10 or 100 Gbps), provide your contact details, and complete the order.

After the order is validated, OVHcloud issues a **Letter of Authorization (LOA)** containing the data centre details needed to install the cross-connect.

## Configure the delivered service

Once the service is delivered, it appears in your Control Panel, where all post-delivery configuration takes place.

Log in to your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink> and open the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> section, then go to <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code>. Select your service to configure it (BGP, static routing, vRack association).

You can also manage your services programmatically using the [OVHcloud API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/console/?section=%2FovhCloudConnect&branch=v1).

```python
import ovh

client = ovh.Client(endpoint='ovh-eu')

# Example: list your existing OVHcloud Connect services
# Check the API console for the exact endpoint and parameters
result = client.get('/ovhCloudConnect')
print(result)
```

Refer to the [Automation guide](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/automation) for API setup instructions.

## What happens after ordering

<img className="thumbnail" alt="What happens after ordering" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-direct/fig-1.svg" loading="lazy" />

1. **Order placed** — You order the service on the OVHcloud website and receive an order confirmation.
2. **LOA delivered** — OVHcloud issues the Letter of Authorization (LOA), which contains the data centre details needed to install the cross-connect.
3. **Cross-connect installed** — Give the LOA to your data centre operator. They install the fibre patch cable. Timing depends on the facility.
4. **Port becomes active** — The service is delivered once OVHcloud detects light on the cross-connect, or 60 days after the order, or by manual arrangement with an OVHcloud agent. The service then appears in the Control Panel, where you can configure BGP and associate it with your vRack.

:::warning

You have **60 days** after the order to finalize the interconnection (order the cross-connect and connect your equipment). Beyond 60 days — even without detected light — the service is considered operational.

:::

## Pricing and billing

- OVHcloud Connect Direct is billed **monthly**.
- Pricing depends on the PoP location and bandwidth tier.
- The initial term starts on effective provisioning of the service, or 30 days after the first LOA is issued, whichever comes first.
- Cross-connect fees from the data centre operator are separate and billed by the operator.
