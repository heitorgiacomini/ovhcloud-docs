## Objective

OVHcloud Connect Provider is the provider-managed option: a third-party network provider builds the physical link to an OVHcloud PoP on your behalf, so you do not need to be present in the same data centre as OVHcloud. It is always a Layer 3 service. This quick start covers the end-to-end path — from ordering to a configured link — so that by the end you have a connection carrying traffic to your OVHcloud resources.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- ✅ An **OVHcloud account** with billing configured
- ✅ A contract or account with a **supported provider** (see [Providers](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/providers))
- ✅ A **router** that supports BGP peering (OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a Layer 3 service)
- ✅ A planned **IP addressing scheme** and **ASN**

Log in to your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>, open the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> section, then go to <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code> and select your solution.

## Steps overview

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview of the steps to set up OVHcloud Connect Provider from the Control Panel" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-provider-control-panel-setup/fig-1.svg" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1 — Order OVHcloud Connect Provider

1. Select <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect Provider</code>.
2. Choose your **provider**, **PoP location**, and **bandwidth**.
3. Review pricing and submit the order.
4. OVHcloud generates a **service key** for your provider.

For detailed steps, see [Order OVHcloud Connect Provider](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-provider).

### Step 2 — Share the service key

Give the **service key** to your provider. This key allows the provider to identify and activate the connection to OVHcloud on your behalf.

In your provider's portal, create the connection to OVHcloud and enter the service key. See the [Providers](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/providers) guide.

### Step 3 — Provider provisions the connection

The provider sets up the physical and logical link between their infrastructure and OVHcloud's PoP. This is usually fast (minutes to hours for on-demand providers), but timing varies.

You can track the connection status in both the **OVHcloud Control Panel** and your **provider's portal**.

### Step 4 — Configure your service in the OVHcloud Control Panel

Once your solution shows as "Active", open it from the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> > <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code> section and configure it. OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a **Layer 3** service.

#### Associate a vRack

Click <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code> and select an existing vRack from the drop-down menu. A message confirms the association.

See [Associate an OVHcloud Connect to your vRack](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/associate-vrack).

#### Add a PoP configuration (L3)

In the `PoP configuration` menu, click <code className="action">Add a PoP configuration</code>, select the L3 configuration, and enter:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer ASN | Your BGP AS number, configured on your router located in the PoP |
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the PoP |
| Subnetwork in /30 | A /30 IPv4 block, used for the link between your router and the OVHcloud router in the PoP |

#### Add an availability zone (AZ) configuration

Under the `AZ configuration` menu, click <code className="action">Add a configuration</code>, select an availability zone, and enter:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers in the AZ (may differ from the PoP ASN) |
| A /28 subnetwork | A private subnetwork configured in your vRack in the selected AZ (an IPv4 block of /28 or larger) |

#### Add a routing configuration

Click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the availability zone, then <code className="action">Add routing configuration</code>, and choose **BGP** or **Static**:

- **BGP** — enter your **Customer ASN** and the **IP Neighbour** (the BGP neighbour IP of your router in the AZ, within the AZ subnetwork).
- **Static** — enter the **Network Focus** (a prefix in CIDR notation) and the **Next hop** (a gateway IP within the subnetwork range).

### Step 5 — Verify BGP session and routing

OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a Layer 3 service — BGP peering is established between your router (or your provider's router, depending on your provider offering) and the OVHcloud PoP. Verify in the OVHcloud Control Panel and in your provider's portal that the session is established and routes are being exchanged.

See [Configure OCC L3 with BGP](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-bgp) for detailed guidance.

### Step 6 — Test and verify

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| **Connection status** | Shows "Active" in OVHcloud Control Panel and provider portal |
| **BGP session** | Established — check on your router and in the OVHcloud Control Panel |
| **Routes** | Your prefixes visible on OVHcloud side; OVHcloud routes visible on your side |
| **Ping** | Ping an OVHcloud resource from your network |
| **Traceroute** | Verify traffic goes through the private link, not the Internet |

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to check |
|---|---|
| Connection stuck in "Pending" | Verify the service key was entered correctly on the provider's side |
| Provider shows "Active" but OVHcloud shows "Down" | Contact OVHcloud support — there may be a provisioning delay |
| BGP session not establishing | Check peering IPs, ASN, and VLAN ID on both your router and the OVHcloud PoP configuration |
| High latency or packet loss | Check provider's network status page; run traceroute to identify where delays occur |
