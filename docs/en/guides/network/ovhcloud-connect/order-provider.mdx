## Objective

This guide explains how to order an OVHcloud Connect service through a **third-party network provider** (e.g. Megaport, Equinix Fabric, Console Connect).

## What you will need

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | The third-party provider you want to use (see [Providers](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/providers)) |
| **PoP location** | The OVHcloud PoP where the provider will connect |
| **Bandwidth** | The desired speed (from 50 Mbps to 50 Gbps — options vary by provider) |
| **Your ASN & IP plan** | OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a Layer 3 service; you need an ASN and peering IPs for BGP |

The **bandwidth** available for OVHcloud Connect Provider ranges from 50 Mbps to 50 Gbps (options vary by provider).

## Place your order

OVHcloud Connect Provider is ordered from the [OVHcloud website](https://www.ovhcloud.com/), not from the Control Panel. Select the OVHcloud Connect Provider offer, choose your **PoP location** and **bandwidth**, and complete the order.

Once the order is validated, OVHcloud emails you a **service key**. This key identifies your OVHcloud Connect order and lets your provider provision the connection.

:::warning
**Important:** Keep the service key — you will need it in the next step.
:::

## Enter the service key in your provider's portal

In your provider's portal, enter the service key provided by OVHcloud, then create the connection to OVHcloud. Refer to your provider's documentation for the exact steps (for example [Megaport](https://docs.megaport.com/), [Equinix Fabric](https://docs.equinix.com/fabric/), or [Console Connect](https://www.consoleconnect.com/help/)).

## What happens after ordering

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Provisioning steps after ordering: service key generated, provider provisions the link, then connection activated" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-provider/fig-1.svg" loading="lazy" />

1. **Service key emailed** — OVHcloud sends you the service key after you place the order.
2. **Provider provisions** — Your provider uses the service key to set up the virtual or physical link.
3. **Link becomes active** — OVHcloud activates the connection and the status changes to "Active" in the Control Panel.
4. **Configure and test** — Verify BGP peering, associate with your vRack, and test connectivity.

## Pricing and billing

- **OVHcloud** bills you for the OVHcloud Connect Provider service (monthly).
- **Your provider** bills you separately for their circuit/VXC (pricing varies by provider, bandwidth, and contract).
- Ensure you understand both billing structures before ordering.
