## Objective

This guide walks you through the essential steps to establish a **direct, private connection** between your infrastructure and OVHcloud — without using a third-party provider.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- ✅ An **OVHcloud account** with billing configured
- ✅ Equipment (or a circuit) in a **data centre where OVHcloud has a PoP** (see [PoP Locations](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/pop-locations-regions))
- ✅ A **router** that supports BGP and single-mode fibre optic interfaces
- ✅ A planned **IP addressing scheme** (peering IPs and prefixes to advertise)
- ✅ A private or public **ASN** (Autonomous System Number)

Log in to your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>, open the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> section, then go to <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code> and select your solution.

## Steps overview

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview of the steps to set up OVHcloud Connect Direct from the Control Panel" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel-setup/fig-1.svg" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1 — Order OVHcloud Connect Direct

1. Select <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect Direct</code>.
2. Choose your **PoP location** and **bandwidth** (e.g. 1, 10 or 100 Gbps).
3. Fill in technical details: your ASN, contact information, and any special instructions for the data centre.
4. Review pricing and confirm the order.

For detailed ordering steps, see [Order OVHcloud Connect Direct](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-direct).

### Step 2 — Receive the LOA

After your order is validated, OVHcloud sends you a **Letter of Authorization (LOA)** by email. This document contains:

- The data centre and rack/cage reference for OVHcloud's equipment
- The port designation on OVHcloud's side
- Instructions for the data centre operator

### Step 3 — Install the physical cross-connect

Give the LOA to your **data centre operator** (or handle it yourself if you manage your own cage). They will install a fibre-optic patch cable between your router port and OVHcloud's designated port.

This step may take a few days depending on the data centre's processes.

### Step 4 — Associate with your vRack

Link your OVHcloud Connect service to your **vRack** so that your OVHcloud resources (servers, VMs, etc.) can communicate over the private connection.

See [Associate an OVHcloud Connect to your vRack](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/associate-vrack).

### Step 5 — Configure your service in the OVHcloud Control Panel

Once the physical link is up, open your solution from the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> > <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code> section and configure it.

#### Associate a vRack

If you have not done so already, click <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code> and select an existing vRack from the drop-down menu. A message confirms the association.

#### Add a PoP configuration

In the `PoP configuration` menu, click <code className="action">Add a PoP configuration</code> and choose the level:

- **L2** — select L2 from the drop-down menu and confirm. This is a transparent point-to-point link (one PoP, one availability zone).
- **L3** — select L3 and enter:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer ASN | Your BGP AS number, configured on your router located in the PoP |
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the PoP |
| Subnetwork in /30 | A /30 IPv4 block, used for the link between your router and the OVHcloud router in the PoP |

#### Add an availability zone (AZ) configuration

Under the `AZ configuration` menu, click <code className="action">Add a configuration</code>, select an availability zone, and (for L3) enter:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers in the AZ (may differ from the PoP ASN) |
| A /28 subnetwork | A private subnetwork configured in your vRack in the selected AZ (an IPv4 block of /28 or larger) |

#### Add a routing configuration

Click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the availability zone, then <code className="action">Add routing configuration</code>, and choose **BGP** or **Static**:

- **BGP** — enter your **Customer ASN** and the **IP Neighbour** (the BGP neighbour IP of your router in the AZ, within the AZ subnetwork).
- **Static** — enter the **Network Focus** (a prefix in CIDR notation) and the **Next hop** (a gateway IP within the subnetwork range).

You can add several routing configurations in the same availability zone; the type (BGP or Static) chosen for the first one applies to the others.

Then, on **your router**, configure the matching BGP session (or static routes) and advertise the prefixes you want to reach from OVHcloud. For detailed routing guidance, see [Configure OCC L3 with BGP](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-bgp) or [Configure OCC L3 with static routes](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-static).

### Step 6 — Test and verify

Run these checks to confirm everything is working:

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| **Physical link** | Confirm the port shows as "up" in the OVHcloud Control Panel and on your router. |
| **BGP session** | Verify the session is **Established** in the OVHcloud Control Panel and on your router. |
| **Route exchange** | Confirm routes from OVHcloud appear in your routing table and vice versa. |
| **Connectivity** | Ping a known OVHcloud resource (e.g. a VM in your vRack) from your on-premises network. |
| **Traceroute** | Run `traceroute` to confirm traffic takes the private path (not the internet). |

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to check |
|---|---|
| Port shows "down" | Physical cable, SFP/transceiver compatibility, data centre cross-connect status |
| BGP session stuck in "Active" or "Idle" | Peer IP addresses, ASN configuration, firewall rules (TCP port 179) |
| No routes exchanged | BGP prefix filters, route-map configuration, maximum-prefix limits |
| Cannot ping OVHcloud resources | vRack association, VLAN ID, subnet configuration in AZ |

If you cannot resolve the issue, [open a support ticket](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/incident-followup) with your BGP output, interface status, and traceroute results.
