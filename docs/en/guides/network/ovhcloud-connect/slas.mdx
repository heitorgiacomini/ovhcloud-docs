## Objective

A **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** is a contractual commitment from OVHcloud that defines the guaranteed level of service for OVHcloud Connect. SLAs give you confidence that your connection will meet specific performance and availability targets.

## What the SLA covers

The OVHcloud Connect SLA is a **monthly availability** commitment. The level you are entitled to depends on how your connections are configured, as detailed in the table below.

:::info
**Scope:** The SLA covers the OVHcloud portion of the service only, from the Point of Presence (PoP) to the datacenter. It excludes the third-party Virtual Circuit provided through a Provider connection. There is no latency SLA and no response-time or resolution-time commitment.
:::

## SLA levels and configuration

The availability commitment depends on your configuration:

| Configuration | Monthly availability SLA |
|---|---|
| 1 connection on 1 PoP | No commitment |
| 2 connections on 1 PoP ("Local redundant connection") | No commitment |
| 2 connections on 2 PoPs ("High Availability – Level 1") | 99.9% |
| 4 connections on 2 PoPs to 2 datacentres ("High Availability – Level 2") | 99.99% |

Only redundant configurations across two PoPs qualify for an availability commitment. A single connection, or two connections within a single PoP, carries no availability commitment.

## SLA prerequisites

For the SLA to be valid, you must:

1. **Follow OVHcloud's configuration guidelines** — Incorrect BGP configuration or unsupported setups may void the SLA.
2. **Report incidents promptly** — Use the official support channels (see [Declare and follow up upon an incident](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/incident-followup)).
3. **Accept scheduled maintenance** — OVHcloud may perform maintenance windows. Downtime during announced maintenance is typically excluded from SLA calculations.
4. **Use a supported architecture** — Higher SLA tiers require redundant connections. A single connection cannot claim the 99.99% SLA.

## Remedies and service credits

If OVHcloud fails to meet the availability SLA, you may be entitled to **service credits** — a percentage of the impacted service's monthly cost, based on the measured availability:

| Measured monthly availability | Service credit |
|---|---|
| 99.9% down to 99% | 10% |
| 99% down to 95% | 20% |
| Below 95% | 30% |

Service credits are capped at 30% of the impacted service's monthly cost per month. Claims must be submitted within one month of the closure of the corresponding support ticket.

The exact credit calculation and claim process are described in the OVHcloud Connect specific conditions, available in your contract or on the OVHcloud website.

## Monitoring your connection

OVHcloud Connect provides on-demand diagnostics rather than a continuous uptime dashboard or alerting feature. To check the state of your connection, run the available diagnostics from the Control Panel or the API.

See [Monitor your OVHcloud Connect](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/monitor) for detailed guidance on the on-demand diagnostics available.
